Northeast Pest Solution

Guwahati’s professional pest management team

Northeast Pest Solution protects homes, offices and commercial properties from unwanted pests through professional pest management. Led by proprietor Jugal Kalita, our trained technicians handle termites, cockroaches, bed bugs, mosquitoes, rodents and ants.

Every job begins with an inspection to find the source of the problem. We then apply safe, effective treatment, set up long-term protection and follow up so the pests do not come back.

Our treatments are eco-friendly and odourless, and safe for your family, your children and your pets. Our goal is simple: a pest-free, healthy and happy space for every customer.
